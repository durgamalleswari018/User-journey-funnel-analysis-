 User Journey Funnel Analysis
 
📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the e-commerce customer journey from website visits to completed purchases. The goal is to identify customer drop-off points, understand conversion behavior, and discover opportunities to improve revenue and customer experience.

🎯 Objectives
- Analyze the complete customer purchase funnel
- Identify major customer drop-off stages
- Compare conversion rates across devices
- Evaluate marketing channel performance
- Analyze campaign effectiveness
- Compare user types and regions
- Track monthly conversion and revenue trends
- Understand the impact of discounts
- Provide actionable business recommendations
  
 🔄 Funnel Stages
Website Visit → Product View → Add to Cart → Checkout → Purchase

📊 Analysis Performed
- Overall Funnel Analysis
- Customer Drop-off Analysis
- Device-wise Conversion Analysis
- Marketing Channel Analysis
- Channel Revenue Analysis
- User Type Analysis
- Region-wise Analysis
- Monthly Funnel Trends
- Monthly Conversion Rate Trends
- Monthly Revenue Trends
- Campaign Performance Analysis
- Discount Impact Analysis
  
💡 Key Business Insights
- Identified important customer drop-off points in the purchase journey.
- Compared conversion performance across different devices.
- Evaluated marketing channels based on conversion and revenue.
- Identified differences in campaign performance.
- Analyzed monthly changes in conversion and revenue.
- Evaluated the relationship between discounts and purchasing behavior.
  
 🚀 Business Recommendations
- Optimize the stages with the highest customer drop-off.
- Improve the experience on lower-performing devices.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing channels.
- Prioritize campaigns with stronger conversion and revenue performance.
- Use monthly trends for better campaign planning.
- Evaluate discounts based on both conversion and revenue impact.
